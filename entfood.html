DINING

Looking for your next stop to try for lunch or dinner? Check out these local favorites!

Seasons Restaurant

A local cafe style American comfort food spot, well-loved by locals. Sunday-Tuesday are breakfast hours, the rest of the week runs through dinner. Known for it's cozy atomosphere, excellent egg breakfasts and steaks, all ingredients sourced from local farmers.

The RIB GUY and GAL

Rustic bar and diner known for it's fantastic BBQ, and beloved fries. Specializes in a wide beer selection and unique house-made cocktails.

ENTERTAINMENT

Planning your next fun destination? Check out local experiences below!

Blue Fox Theater

A historic cinema built in 1929, known for it's timeless, cozy atmosphere. Showings from Thursday to Sundays at 6:30 PM.

Camas Lanes Bowling Alley

The local beloved bowling alley where tournaments are held that additionally offers food.
