Discover local sights and things to do!

Border Days Rodeo

From July 1-4th in Grangeville, enjoy the sights, food, and fun of the Border Days Rodeo!

Highlights: Street Sports Parades on the 2nd, 3rd, and 4th Rodeos starting at 6PM

Farmer's Market

From June to September in Grangeville, every saturday, there's a farmer's market hosted at the local park.

Highlights: All local produce Wide variety Regular community events Local live music

Music in the Park

In Pioneer Park in from July to August at 6PM a series of local concerts are held.

Highlights: Totally free Family friendly Picnics
